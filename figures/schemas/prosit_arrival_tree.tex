\caption{A real ProSiT tree: the arrival model learned from the CTB training
partition (ProSiT 1.0.4, Section~\ref{sec:exp_baseline}). The tree predicts the gap
until the next truck arrives, in open minutes of the arrival calendar, from the
hour and weekday of the previous arrival. Each leaf holds a fitted distribution.
The simulator follows the conditions from the top to one leaf and draws the gap
from that leaf. The means are those of the values the simulator draws. Sunday is
closed in the arrival calendar, so no gaps are drawn there.}
