In preparing this thesis I used the AI assistant Claude Opus 5.5 by Anthropic
\cite{Anthropic2026Claude}, through the tool Claude Code. The assistant supported
the following tasks.
\begin{itemize}
\item Writing and debugging the Python scripts of the project, among them the
extensions of the ProSiT simulator, the experiment runs and their evaluation.
\item Running the experiments and checking their results against the code and the
data.
\item Searching and summarising literature.
\item Drafting and revising the text of the thesis, its structure and its language.
\end{itemize}
The research questions, the choice of the case, the domain knowledge of the terminal
and every decision on methods, results and their interpretation are mine. The
design of the experiments was developed in dialogue with the assistant. I checked
its outputs and the reported values, and I take full responsibility for the content
of this thesis.
