\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chp:conclusion}

This thesis evaluated how far an automatically discovered, data-aware ProSiT
model can represent truck handling at the HHLA Container Terminal Burchardkai.
The result is neither a failed simulator nor a decision-valid digital twin. It
is a reproducible process simulation with strong structural validity, mixed
historical fidelity, and incomplete physical and counterfactual validity.

\section{Answers to the Research Questions}

\paragraph{RQ1: Historical fidelity.}
The final \textit{rules$+$workload} model reproduces arrivals and yard-service
times more closely than complete truck turnaround. Across ten seeds, the
inter-arrival EMD is 0.072 minutes; yard-service EMD is 3.025 minutes
unweighted and 2.381 minutes when weighted by real activity frequency. Mean
turnaround is 31.63 minutes in simulation and 39.74 minutes in the temporal
hold-out; the corresponding EMD is 8.12 minutes. The simulated P90 is 53.70
minutes rather than 71.00 minutes. The model therefore reproduces selected
input and activity-level distributions but systematically underestimates the
end-to-end KPI.

Two diagnostics delimit this error. Route-specific fifth-percentile
transition baselines lie between 2.16 and 5.28 minutes and sum to a
frequency-weighted 7.62 minutes per visit. This scale is consistent with
omitted movement and other pre-service time, but it is not a case-level or
causal decomposition. In addition, real mean turnaround increases by 2.45
minutes between training and hold-out. Missing physical time and temporal
transfer error are therefore both plausible contributors and cannot be
identified separately from the available timestamps.

\paragraph{RQ2: Control-flow suitability.}
With the explicit event order preserved, Inductive Miner discovers a compact
sequential Petri net without a parallel operator. It reaches held-out fitness
0.999992, 99.994\,\% completely fitting held-out traces, generalisation 0.9413,
and precision 0.7558. The lower precision reflects additional sequential yard
combinations and a silent gate-only bypass, not simultaneous activities of one
truck. All generated reference cases satisfy the hard sequential case
contract. A separate bypass repair raises precision to 0.7939 while reducing
generalisation to 0.8859. The unchanged discovered net is therefore a suitable
simulation backbone, provided that its overgeneralisation is disclosed and
not confused with physical validity.

\paragraph{RQ3: Contextual expressiveness.}
Workload-blind decision rules provide the strongest result in the three-level
ablation. Relative to \textit{no-rules}, they reduce yard-activity-rate L1
error by 0.0103 and suppress the sampled gate-only bypass; the 0.115-minute
turnaround-EMD improvement is unresolved. Adding the available workload state
then increases turnaround EMD by 0.375 minutes, lowers simulated mean
turnaround by 0.576 minutes and P90 by 1.000 minute, and increases activity-rate
L1 error by 0.0598. No weighted yard-service improvement is resolved. More
context is consequently not equivalent to a better process state. The CTB
workload proxies do not replace unobserved movement, queue, and equipment
state.

\paragraph{RQ4: What-if capability.}
Both interventions execute reproducibly, but their technical meanings differ.
Closing T22 changes the feasible resource mapping and calendar; it is a
transparent resource-allocation intervention, not a decision-tree edit. The
20\,\% demand scenario changes the empirical distribution in the arrival
model's single leaf. This matches ProSiT's leaf-value interaction mechanism,
but it does not exercise a context-dependent split or subtree modification.
The thesis therefore demonstrates inspectable parameter intervention, not the
full range of ProSiT tree interactions.

Neither intervention changes mean turnaround by an amount resolved across the
ten paired seeds. Under 20\,\% higher demand, mean RMG service rises by 0.091
minutes and RMG-receive service by 0.073 minutes, but these small component
effects are model-conditional. The stress ladder explains the weak headline
response: the 20\,\% intervention remains below the model's nominal RMG
saturation region. At a configured twofold intensity, the realised arrival-rate
ratio is 2.367 and mean turnaround, P90, and RMG pre-service time rise by 4.644,
10.700, and 5.878 minutes, respectively. The simulator can propagate abstract
capacity pressure through pre-service delay, but it does not represent
container location, truck movement, explicit queues, or waterside--landside
equipment allocation. Its scenario outputs are valid model experiments, not
validated forecasts of physical terminal interventions.

The expert-validated limit of three RMGs per block is retained as a physical
eligibility condition. Against the uncapped model, it produces neither a
resolved direct mean-turnaround effect nor a resolved cap-by-scenario
mean-turnaround interaction. The cap prevents an invalid resource state; it
does not create the thesis's headline findings.

\section{Scientific Contribution and Validity Boundary}

The thesis makes four concrete contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item a simulation-ready CTB event-log abstraction that separates explicit
    process order from imperfect timestamp observations;
    \item a temporal held-out and multi-seed evaluation of ProSiT in a physical
    logistics setting;
    \item a layered validity audit that separates control-flow conformance,
    stochastic fidelity, contextual transfer, physical constraints, and
    scenario validity; and
    \item a reproducible distinction between discovered parameters,
    expert-supplied constraints, and scenario-specific interventions.
\end{enumerate}

The central finding is that automatic simulation-model generation transfers
successfully where the event log observes the relevant process relation. It
captures sequential control flow, arrivals, and parts of activity timing. It
reaches its current boundary where the operational mechanism is absent from
the log: movement, location, queues, equipment interaction, and changing
physical capacity. High replay fitness cannot compensate for those omissions,
and adding correlated attributes does not create missing state.

This distinction is operationally important. A validated terminal model could
compare hourly truck-slot limits or handling priorities before implementation.
The present model already provides an auditable intervention-and-measurement
workflow, but its outputs should support diagnosis and model development rather
than unrestricted prescriptive decisions. This conclusion is consistent with
the need for multi-perspective validation identified in recent simulation
research \cite{Sulis2026SimulationReview}.

\section{Future Work}

Future work should focus on the missing mechanisms rather than on additional
Monte-Carlo repetitions of the same abstraction:

\begin{itemize}
    \item separate measured transit time from residual waiting and represent it
    once, using route or position observations to avoid double counting;
    \item add explicit spatial assignment, equipment state, and queue dynamics,
    potentially through a coupled discrete-event, agent-based, or
    object-centric design \cite{VanDerAalst2023OCPM,Knopp2023ObjectCentricSimulation};
    \item evaluate rolling or recency-weighted discovery against later temporal
    windows; and
    \item define an expert-validated, context-dependent operating policy and
    test it through an explicit ProSiT leaf or subtree intervention, followed
    by prospective comparison with observed terminal outcomes.
\end{itemize}
